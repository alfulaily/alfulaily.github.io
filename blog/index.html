In real-world hospital operations governed by the CMS Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP), false negatives carry catastrophic clinical and financial consequences. Closing the Care-Process Gap: Why Unmeasured Lab Tests Are Informative Absence Signals in Longitudinal EHRs

Generic computer science textbooks recommend dropping missing values or applying multivariate imputation (MICE) to fill missing fields. In clinical electronic health records, however, laboratory tests are never Missing Completely at Random (MCAR). When an inpatient record shows A1Cresult == 'None', that missingness is an informative care-process signal reflecting clinical decision-making. Imputing values destroys vital information about diagnostic adherence.
